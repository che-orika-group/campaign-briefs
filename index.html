02 · The Concept

The Mesh Top is an Elevated Basic

It's obviously just a long sleeve shirt.
But it's mesh. It's a little bit see-through.
And that adds a completely different vibe.

The Alise Top is the most versatile piece in your closet. Pair it with a leather mini skirt and it's a going-out top. Throw it over capri pants and it's elevated casual. Wear it with jeans and a structured bag and it's an actual outfit. Three colours, endless pairings — dress it up or dress it down. Shot in-store to keep the focus on the product and the styling.

In-Store Bold Colour Elevated Basic See-Through Confidence Versatile Dress Up / Dress Down
Voiceover Script · Educational Reel

"If you struggle to find good closet basics — just like simple shirts that you can either dress up or dress down, something cute, casual, comfortable, but a little bit more elevated — is this not literally the perfect shirt? Like, it's obviously just a long sleeve shirt, but it's mesh. It's a little bit see-through, so you can see the bra, so I feel like it adds like a completely different vibe to the outfit."

How to film this: Voiceover plays while showing quick cuts of the mesh top paired with different bottoms — leather mini skirt, capri pants, jeans with a cute bag. Each cut is 2-3 seconds. The viewer sees multiple outfit changes in 15 seconds. End with "I wear this all the time" over different outfit pairings.

02 · The Concept

She walked into the market
in a playsuit and heels.
She doesn't fit.
That's the point.

The Diva Playsuit is for the girl who's overdressed everywhere she goes — and she knows it. Put her in a fruit market, she stands out. Put her in Shoprite, she stands out. Put her on a couch waiting to leave, and the playsuit does everything. Burgundy shimmer, long sleeves, cowl back that flips to the front. One piece, three ways to wear it, and she didn't need anyone to tell her how.

Overdressed Contrast Burgundy Shimmer Body Confidence 3 Ways She Doesn't Fit
03 · One Playsuit, Three Ways

How SheWears It

01
As Designed
Round neck at the front, cowl drape at the back. Covered, clean, the shimmer in the fabric does all the talking. This is the "dinner with his family" version — elegant but the playsuit shape still shows body.
02
Flipped
Cowl neck worn at the FRONT. Now you get that draped neckline showing chest and decolletage. The back becomes the plain round neck. Same playsuit, completely different energy — this is the "going out, want people to look" version.
03
Off-Shoulder
Cowl neck at the front but pulled off one shoulder. Shows collarbone, asymmetric, unexpected. This is the third way nobody told you about — the "I styled it myself" moment.
04 · Three Sizes, Three Voices

What Each ModelSays

Model 1
Size Small
5'4 · Petite frame
"So I'm wearing a size small. I'm like 5'4, and honestly the length on this is perfect for me — it hits right here and it doesn't ride up when I walk. I'm wearing it with the cowl neck in the front so it's more covered, and I feel like this is how you'd wear it to like a dinner or something where you want to look put together but you don't want to do too much. The sleeves are long which I love because I don't have to think about what jacket to bring."
Model 2
Size Medium
5'7 · Athletic build
"I'm a medium, 5'7. What I like about this is that you can actually flip it — so right now the cowl neck is at my back and the front is giving more body. Same playsuit, completely different energy. Model 3
Size Large / XL

Each one demonstrates their way of wearing the playsuit while they talk. Quick cuts between them. The vibe is three friends who all own the same piece and wear it differently — not a fashion show. End with all three standing together, same angle as the Sabo reference.

The Concept

She's not doing anything special.
She's just walking. Handbag on her arm.
Crossing the road. That's the content.

No styled photoshoot. No location scouting. Just a girl in the Sophie Dress walking outside in the morning. Nonchalant street energy. She looks good and she's not trying. A few walking shots, a few portrait shots, done.

Creative Concepts

Content IdeasBeyond the Shoots

Standalone content concepts that don't require a full shoot day. Some are ready to film, some need creative development. Each one is a separate content piece.

"What Nobody Sees" (Founder BTS)
Concept Approved / Script Needs Cleanup
40-60 seconds
Founder talking about real daily work. Camera on her, not screens. Website appears naturally twice. Voiceover needs human tone — not scripted-sounding, not presenting, just talking like she's explaining to a friend. The content is her process, her decisions, the parts of running a brand that nobody posts about. This is the "day in my life" version that's actually honest.
The Obsession Concept
Needs Creative Session
30-40 seconds
Girl screenshots CO Instagram post, keeps going back to it, tries to recreate the look from her own closet, can't get it right, finally buys. Needs a visual trick to show the "going back" moment — maybe the phone screen keeps popping up, or she keeps swiping back to the same saved post. Needs honest voiceover — internal monologue, not selling. To be developed with the full team in a creative session.
"The Zoom"
Approved
10 seconds
Phone screen zooms into product photo on the website or Instagram. Match cut to real fabric on model — same angle, same framing. Two shots edited together. Clean, satisfying, works as an ad or organic content. The zoom-in on screen needs to match the zoom-out on the real product perfectly.
"The Tab"
Approved
10 seconds
Screen recording of someone scrolling through Safari tabs. Lots of random tabs open — news, social media, work stuff. One of them is cheorika.com. The scroll pauses on it for a beat. End card: "be honest." or just the logo. Simple, relatable, the kind of thing people screenshot and share.
"The Outfit Was Waiting"
Idea Stage
10 seconds
The outfit has the confidence, not the girl. She goes back and forth — tries different things from her closet, nothing works, puts on the CO piece and everything clicks. The piece was waiting for her to stop overthinking. Needs development — the "back and forth" needs to be visual and quick, not a full try-on montage.
This or That Questions (Alise Tops Campaign)
Approved
10 reels · 10-15 seconds each
Three models, each in a different Alise Top colour. Question pops up on screen. They react — pointing, shaking heads, laughing, picking sides. Quick cuts between questions. Playful energy, not posed. People come for the question, stay for the outfit.

2. Be 18 again knowing what you know now or skip to 40 with everything figured out?
3. Everyone knows your salary or everyone knows your body count?
4. Date someone who loves you more or someone you love more?
5. Marry for love with nothing or marry for comfort without passion?
6. Be the smartest person in the room or the most liked?
7. Rich in Nigeria or average abroad?
8. Have your dream career but no time or an average job with all the free time?
9. Be beautiful with no talent or talented with average looks?
10. Never eat jollof rice again or never eat suya again?
